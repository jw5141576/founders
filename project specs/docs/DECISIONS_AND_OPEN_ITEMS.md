# Decisions and open items — The Founders v1.1

## Confirmed project decisions
- The Founders is the parent organisation, created by eleven original founding members.
- Each original founder has an equal founding interest in everything The Founders owns, controls and produces.
- Display each founding interest as 9.1%. The displayed total of 100.1% is accepted as a rounding convention.
- Store equal ownership units (one unit per founder; eleven units total) as the calculation basis. Do not allocate money or votes using the rounded displayed percentages.
- New members do not automatically receive retrospective founding interests or prior venture equity.
- Ventures are child organisations under The Founders, with separate operational records and separate bank accounts.
- Parent reporting consolidates venture information without treating inter-organisation transfers as external revenue.
- Currency KES; base financial year January–December.
- Constitution takes precedence over Master MOU; Master MOU takes precedence over a Specific Venture Annexure.
- Executive bank signatories: Chairperson, Secretary and Treasurer; at least two approvals are required for applicable withdrawals.
- Loan policy: 10% one-time interest on original principal; standard 30-day term; 5% recurring monthly penalty on original principal after default; penalty does not compound; two-month new-member waiting period.
- Member investments are tracked separately from chama contributions.
- Double-entry accounting is required.
- General members may view approved group-level reports, not other members' private financial information.
- Secretary manages subsidiary administration; Supervisor/Operations Lead manages day-to-day operations. Confirm the final title in the Constitution and MOU before production role configuration.
- Welfare event contribution values: KSh 1,000 for death of parent/spouse/child; KSh 500 for baby shower/birthday, subject to the adopted mandatory policy.

## Venture annexure capabilities
- Venture identity, project code, operating location, setup date and launch date.
- Capital raised and category-level allocation: premises, facility equipment, tools/logistics, compliance/branding, communications, working capital and unallocated reserve.
- Individual partner capital contributions.
- Dedicated venture banking and two-of-three executive signatory approval.
- Founding partner compensation and external payroll, with the stated KSh 15,000 baseline net salary as a configurable policy value.
- Venture life stage and reinvestment rate configuration.
- Emergency reserve tracking at 10% of revenue, as stated in the Master MOU.

## Open items before production
1. Confirm legal form and registration status of The Founders and each venture; confirm whether the 11 equal founding interests are legally enforceable ownership/economic rights and how they apply to assets, liabilities, distributions and exit.
2. The documents use both “Supervisor” and “Operations Lead”. Select one canonical title.
3. Constitution says simple-majority elections; Master MOU has simple majority for routine decisions and two-thirds for specified structural decisions. Implement per decision category and final signed documents.
4. Master MOU quorum text gives regular meetings 50% + 1 and AGM 60% to 75%; define one exact AGM quorum.
5. Loan penalty start date and monthly charging convention need exact wording (e.g. first charge on due date + 1 day, then monthly anniversaries).
6. Clarify whether repayment allocation may be directed freely among principal, interest and penalties, and what happens if allocations do not cover accrued amounts.
7. Define financial approval thresholds and which transactions require two signatories versus ordinary workflow approval.
8. Define how partner founder compensation is calculated, paid and recorded.
9. Define profit distribution policy alongside reinvestment ranges and 10% revenue reserve.
10. Define exit valuation method, treatment of uncalled capital, liabilities, and transfer restrictions.
11. Define privacy roles, retention, backup, disaster recovery and hosting region.
12. Add formal journal posting function, debit/credit balance enforcement, immutable posted entries, reversal workflow, row-level security and cross-organisation consistency checks before production.

## Important implementation note
This is a structured v1.1 baseline for requirements and database development, not legal advice, a statutory ownership register, or a production-hardened financial system.
